Northport, a village inside the Town of Huntington.

Northport, a village since 1894 in Huntington town (Suffolk County), has no town of the same name. East Northport is a different hamlet, so a caller with that name on the mail should say so. We hold one code for the village, 11768.

Village status does not alter how rodents enter a building, only the name on the mailing label. A technician starts with the roof edge, vents and foundation of the house you describe, whatever the label says.

What a long winter does to a building.

Walk the outside of the house in early fall and again after the first hard freeze. Look for fresh soil, loosened covers and leaves piled against siding.

A mild winter does not mean a quiet one. Animals that stay active all year keep probing gaps, and food left outdoors keeps them close.

Walking through a visit.

The visit begins with a slow walk around the outside, looking at the roof edge, vents, pipe entries, garage door and foundation. Then the technician goes inside to the attic, basement or whichever space has signs.

Photos help the plan. Taken with a coin for scale before any cleanup, they show how big the droppings are and where the activity concentrates.

Your mail's place name versus your own walls.

Cleanup in the right order

A short pause before touching anything protects your lungs for next to nothing.

  • Scrub lightly. Heavy elbow work throws dust and defeats the purpose of wetting.
  • Closed rooms keep their air, so let a shut-up space exchange it before you walk in.
  • Skip bare hands entirely, because waste on a fingertip can end up in a mouth.
  • Every dropping and stain should be dripping, not damp.
  • Double-bag when the contents are wet or have sharp edges.
  • Pay attention to your health over the following weeks and report flu-like symptoms to a doctor.
