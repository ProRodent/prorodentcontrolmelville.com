A handful of quick steps.

  • Keep out of attics and crawl spaces, where shredded insulation puts dust in the air.
  • Do not disturb a nest, as a frightened parent may flee deeper into the framing.
  • Find the attic hatch and cellar door and keep both reachable.
  • Work out which outside wall the sounds seem to track.

A quick damp-cleaning guide

Going slowly keeps fine particles out of your breathing.

  • Trade the broom for a trigger sprayer.
  • Open the windows and the doors, step out, and give the room thirty minutes to clear.
  • Skip bare hands entirely, because waste on a fingertip can end up in a mouth.
  • Cover the waste and a margin around it with liquid, take a short break, then start wiping.
  • Tie the bag tight and let it ride out with the usual household trash.
  • Not feeling well after contact with droppings is a question for a physician.
