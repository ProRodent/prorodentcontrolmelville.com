Dos and don'ts for droppings

These lines boil down published health guidance, and they stay true even if you never book a visit.

  • Rule one for droppings: nothing dry. A broom or a shop vacuum sends old waste airborne.
  • Open the windows and the doors, step out, and give the room thirty minutes to clear.
  • Anything soiled is contaminated, so glove up and scrub afterward.
  • Soak, wait, then collect with paper towels.
  • A dead rodent never touches bare skin; the bag goes in the bin.
  • If you get sick, say you were near rodents when you see your doctor, because we can speak only to the pest side.
