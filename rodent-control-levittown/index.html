Small steps before a visit

Going slowly keeps fine particles out of your breathing.

  • Put the dustpan down. Sweeping crumbled droppings is how people end up breathing them.
  • The first thirty minutes are for fresh air alone, with nobody working.
  • Keep gloved hands away from eyes, nose and mouth until they are off and washed.
  • Spray first, wipe afterward, and let about five minutes pass between the two.
  • A dead rodent never touches bare skin; the bag goes in the bin.
  • Diagnosing illness is not our trade, so anyone unwell after contact should consult a clinician.
