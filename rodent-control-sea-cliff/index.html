Small steps before a visit

Going slowly keeps fine particles out of your breathing.

  • A dry rag is no safer than a broom, since it stirs the same powder.
  • The CDC puts the airing time at about thirty minutes before any work starts in a closed space.
  • Skip bare hands entirely, because waste on a fingertip can end up in a mouth.
  • Cover the waste and a margin around it with liquid, take a short break, then start wiping.
  • Double-bag when the contents are wet or have sharp edges.
  • Not feeling well after contact with droppings is a question for a physician.
