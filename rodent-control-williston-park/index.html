Why cold weather matters here.

Heating a house draws warm air out through small gaps, and the draft carries scent. That trail can lead animals to the very opening you never noticed.

Thaws and heavy rain also move animals. Wet ground pushes burrowing rats toward higher, drier spots, and a foundation edge can be exactly that.

Facts about the building let us load the right gear. Phoning from Williston Park? Try to cover these points:

  • A shed, deck or porch that sits right against the foundation.
  • Vents, dryer ducts or pipe collars that look loose or uncovered.
