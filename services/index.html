How the phases connect.

Few jobs stay on one page. A typical sequence starts with a survey, moves to trapping, then to gap closing and a return visit, and ends with cleanout and repairs, since fresh batting spreads out over a nest only gets soiled again.

Every phase has its own quote. That lets you approve trapping and sealing this week and leave cleanout or repair until it suits you.

About the service pages.

Can I order only one of these?

Yes, every service stands alone. If related work looks useful, the technician will tell you.

Do you take on raccoons or squirrels?

These pages cover mice and rats alone. Should the survey turn up some other creature, you hear that on the spot and get pointed elsewhere.

Where do I begin?

Usually with the survey, since it identifies the species and its route into the house. Closing gaps waits until trapping is done, so no animal is left shut inside a wall.
