Small steps before a visit

A short pause before touching anything protects your lungs for next to nothing.

  • Rule one for droppings: nothing dry. A broom or a shop vacuum sends old waste airborne.
  • Weeks of stillness behind a closed door call for a good cross-breeze.
  • Keep gloved hands away from eyes, nose and mouth until they are off and washed.
  • Every dropping and stain should be dripping, not damp.
  • Slide a carcass into a bag without letting skin touch it.
  • Fever, muscle pain or hard breathing after a cleanup mean a doctor visit, and the doctor should hear what you handled. We are a pest company, not a clinic.
