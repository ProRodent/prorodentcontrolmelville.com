Facts that steer the first walk-through.

Before you dial from Woodbury, a minute of looking around pays off. Check these places:

  • A compost bin or brush pile close to a door.
  • Any garage door with a gap at the corners or a worn bottom seal.
  • A shared wall, fence or driveway with the property next door.
  • Piles of firewood, lumber or boxes stacked against an outside wall.

Handling dust the careful way

Calling us is optional for this part. Health departments hand out the same instructions to anyone who finds droppings in a building.

  • Scrub lightly. Heavy elbow work throws dust and defeats the purpose of wetting.
  • Musty air trapped in a sealed shed is the thing to flush out first, so throw it open and stay clear.
  • Gloves first: rubber, vinyl or latex. Hands get washed with soap as the last step.
  • Soak, wait, then collect with paper towels.
  • One bag for all of it, closed before you leave the room.
  • Short of breath or running a fever after exposure? Call a doctor.
