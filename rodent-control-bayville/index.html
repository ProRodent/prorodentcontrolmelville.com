Droppings, minus the broom

Waste from rodents can spread illness, which is why cleanup should be damp, unhurried and quiet. The agencies named below stand behind every line.

  • A dry rag is no safer than a broom, since it stirs the same powder.
  • Wedge a door, let the breeze run through and look at your watch.
  • Skin should not meet droppings, urine or nest material at any point.
  • Spray until glossy, then walk away briefly.
  • Bag it: towels, droppings and any dead animal, picked up with tongs or a gloved hand.
