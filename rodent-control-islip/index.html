How seasons drive animals inside.

Leaf drifts and brush piles give shelter all year, but they matter most just before the cold. Moving them away from the wall removes a covered path.

Walk the outside of the house in early fall and again after the first hard freeze. Look for fresh soil, loosened covers and leaves piled against siding.

Handling dust the careful way

These lines boil down published health guidance, and they stay true even if you never book a visit.

  • Scrub lightly. Heavy elbow work throws dust and defeats the purpose of wetting.
  • Musty air trapped in a sealed shed is the thing to flush out first, so throw it open and stay clear.
  • Anything soiled is contaminated, so glove up and scrub afterward.
  • Drench the droppings using a disinfectant or diluted bleach, then leave the liquid to work for a few minutes.
  • One bag for all of it, closed before you leave the room.
  • Whether a symptom is connected to rodents is for a medical professional to decide.
