Frost, thaw and your walls.

Heating a house draws warm air out through small gaps, and the draft carries scent. That trail can lead animals to the very opening you never noticed.

In deep cold, rodents rarely stay outdoors by choice. The first warm building on their route gets tested, which is why gaps matter more than neatness.

Cleanup in the right order

Going slowly keeps fine particles out of your breathing.

  • Filters leak at the microscopic level, so even a good vacuum gets left in the closet.
  • Wedge a door, let the breeze run through and look at your watch.
  • Single-use gloves are acceptable, followed by a proper hand wash.
  • Soak, wait, then collect with paper towels.
  • When the bag is knotted and binned, wash your hands one more time.
  • If you feel strange later, mention the cleanup to your doctor.
