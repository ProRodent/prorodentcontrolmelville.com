Wet methods only

The point of this list is to hold airborne dust down until help arrives.

  • Put the dustpan down. Sweeping crumbled droppings is how people end up breathing them.
  • Closed rooms keep their air, so let a shut-up space exchange it before you walk in.
  • Skip bare hands entirely, because waste on a fingertip can end up in a mouth.
  • Soak, wait, then collect with paper towels.
  • Slide a carcass into a bag without letting skin touch it.
  • Fever, muscle pain or hard breathing after a cleanup mean a doctor visit, and the doctor should hear what you handled. We are a pest company, not a clinic.
