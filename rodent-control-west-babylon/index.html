What a long winter does to a building.

Walk the outside of the house in early fall and again after the first hard freeze. Look for fresh soil, loosened covers and leaves piled against siding.

Thaws and heavy rain also move animals. Wet ground pushes burrowing rats toward higher, drier spots, and a foundation edge can be exactly that.

Dos and don'ts for droppings

Waste from rodents can spread illness, which is why cleanup should be damp, unhurried and quiet. The agencies named below stand behind every line.

  • Rule one for droppings: nothing dry. A broom or a shop vacuum sends old waste airborne.
  • Weeks of stillness behind a closed door call for a good cross-breeze.
  • Anything soiled is contaminated, so glove up and scrub afterward.
  • Contact time matters with disinfectant, so apply plenty and resist the urge to hurry.
  • Slide a carcass into a bag without letting skin touch it.
  • If you feel strange later, mention the cleanup to your doctor.
