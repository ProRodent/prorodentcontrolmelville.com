Frost, thaw and your walls.

Thaws and heavy rain also move animals. Wet ground pushes burrowing rats toward higher, drier spots, and a foundation edge can be exactly that.

  • A vehicle that has sat for weeks and now smells odd.

Dos and don'ts for droppings

These lines boil down published health guidance, and they stay true even if you never book a visit.

  • Scrub lightly. Heavy elbow work throws dust and defeats the purpose of wetting.
  • Musty air trapped in a sealed shed is the thing to flush out first, so throw it open and stay clear.
  • Gloves first: rubber, vinyl or latex. Hands get washed with soap as the last step.
  • Spray first, wipe afterward, and let about five minutes pass between the two.
  • A dead rodent never touches bare skin; the bag goes in the bin.
  • Whether a symptom is connected to rodents is for a medical professional to decide.
