Cleanup in the right order

The point of this list is to hold airborne dust down until help arrives.

  • Sweeping fast feels efficient and fills the room with drifting specks.
  • The CDC puts the airing time at about thirty minutes before any work starts in a closed space.
  • Gloves first: rubber, vinyl or latex. Hands get washed with soap as the last step.
  • The soak keeps dust on the ground where it belongs, so do not skip it.
  • When the bag is knotted and binned, wash your hands one more time.
  • If you feel strange later, mention the cleanup to your doctor.
