Wet weather and wandering rodents.

Thaws and heavy rain also move animals. Wet ground pushes burrowing rats toward higher, drier spots, and a foundation edge can be exactly that.

Cold snaps are the best-known trigger. Once nights drop, mice and rats start testing buildings for warmth, and a loose vent cover or a gap by a pipe becomes an invitation.

Handling dust the careful way

Going slowly keeps fine particles out of your breathing.

  • Filters leak at the microscopic level, so even a good vacuum gets left in the closet.
  • The CDC puts the airing time at about thirty minutes before any work starts in a closed space.
  • Leave gloves on until the job is done, then remove them inside out and wash.
  • Every dropping and stain should be dripping, not damp.
  • Tie the bag tight and let it ride out with the usual household trash.
  • Not feeling well after contact with droppings is a question for a physician.
