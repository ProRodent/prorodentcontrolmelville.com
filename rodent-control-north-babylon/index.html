Ahead of the technician's arrival.

  • Move kids and pets to the end of the house furthest from the trouble.

Health agency advice in brief

A short pause before touching anything protects your lungs for next to nothing.

  • Put the dustpan down. Sweeping crumbled droppings is how people end up breathing them.
  • Weeks of stillness behind a closed door call for a good cross-breeze.
  • Covered hands while you work, clean hands when you finish.
  • Cover the waste and a margin around it with liquid, take a short break, then start wiping.
  • Double-bag when the contents are wet or have sharp edges.
  • Pay attention to your health over the following weeks and report flu-like symptoms to a doctor.
