Wet weather and wandering rodents.

In deep cold, rodents rarely stay outdoors by choice. The first warm building on their route gets tested, which is why gaps matter more than neatness.

Leaf drifts and brush piles give shelter all year, but they matter most just before the cold. Moving them away from the wall removes a covered path.

Small steps before a visit

Going slowly keeps fine particles out of your breathing.

  • Put the dustpan down. Sweeping crumbled droppings is how people end up breathing them.
  • Closed rooms keep their air, so let a shut-up space exchange it before you walk in.
  • Keep gloved hands away from eyes, nose and mouth until they are off and washed.
  • Drench the droppings using a disinfectant or diluted bleach, then leave the liquid to work for a few minutes.
  • One bag for all of it, closed before you leave the room.
  • If you feel strange later, mention the cleanup to your doctor.
